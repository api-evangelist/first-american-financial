First American Financial Corporation (NYSE: FAF) is a leading provider of title insurance, settlement services, property data and risk solutions for real estate and mortgage transactions, and one of the largest title insurance underwriters in the United States. Its public API surface is the Digital Gateway (developer.firstam.io), a 3scale-managed developer portal operated by First American Mortgage Solutions and First American Data & Analytics, which publishes 16 machine-readable specifications covering 93 operations: title and escrow order management with a 21-event webhook catalogue, nationwide property and ownership data, occupancy, identity and SSN verification, bankruptcy, IRS 4506-C tax transcripts, reverse phone and address search, SCRA military-service checks, liens and judgments in separate FCRA and non-FCRA contracts, OFAC and agency watchlist screening, and NMLS license verification. A second, separate request-access portal at developer.firstam.com fronts the same title and settlement integration. Access to every API is approval-gated: an administrator reviews each account, credentials are issued per App as an App ID and App Key pair, and production access additionally requires the First American relationship team and may require a contract or SOW.

**Areas:** Title Insurance · Real Estate · Settlement Services · Financial Services · Mortgage · Property Data · Identity Verification · Regulatory Compliance · Fortune 1000
